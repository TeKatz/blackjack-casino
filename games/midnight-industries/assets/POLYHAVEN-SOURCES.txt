License for both assets: CC0 1.0 / Public Domain dedication.
https://polyhaven.com/license

Both textures are bundled for offline Windows play. They are sampled only by
the world-ground shader; the original industrial atlas, ore sprites, machines,
water and UI graphics retain their existing sources and artwork.

Refresh these files explicitly with tools/import_polyhaven_terrain.py.
No texture assets are downloaded by the game at runtime.
