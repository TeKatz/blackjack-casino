# Midnight Descent — content and progression

Runs reset all run relics, health and stats to the chosen loadout plus purchased permanent ranks. Soul essence is awarded exactly once on victory or defeat; abandoned runs do not pay. Local Godot user data stores unlocks, ranks, discoveries and statistics, separate from casino credits.

Four loadouts: Warden Guardian, Executioner, Dealer Cardsharp, Joker. Dealer uses ranged cards and spread specials; Executioner uses heavy sword and area sweep; Joker rolls special damage. The active slot (Q) heals on a 24-second cooldown. Four permanent upgrade tracks; 16 relics, including splitting, piercing, echo, burn/lightning, leech, dash damage, crit, attack tempo and active heal upgrades. Projectiles are capped at 180 and pierce at 5. The existing five-room expedition remains intact. Future milestones: larger branching dungeons, distinct new enemy families, additional characters and item art.

Local save: user://descent_progress.cfg. Menu and run smoke exercise progression in memory only.
