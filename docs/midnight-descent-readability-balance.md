# Readability and difficulty polish
Fixed footer wrapping with explicit horizontal sizing; all eight loadouts are shown in a grid and selectable for inspection even when locked. Separate start/unlock action and detailed stats. Decor placement reserves full footprints of fixtures, spike lanes, walls and gate; reduced density and ambient brightness. Layered reactive HUD (health, recovery, shards, kills, cooldown completion). Difficulty: enemy health +25/50/75%, bosses additional 25%, larger groups, floor damage scaling, capped armor mitigation; reduced heal and passive recovery. Essence is based on cleared combat rooms, bosses and elites rather than visited nodes or remaining shards, with no reward for an instant zero-kill failure. Existing saves are preserved.
